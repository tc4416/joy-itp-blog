#### Continue of my TouchDesigner project
I have always wanted to make a physical clock that only shows time when people are not looking at it. I got to try this out using TouchDesigner during summer and made this pice. For my final, I am thinking make the physical version of this happen. I was thinking using frosted acrylic (as i always do lol), and have the clock face moving close and far away from the acrylic to create blurry effect.
<div align = "center">
<img src = "media/time/time-final.gif" width = 400px>
</div>
When I was doing research I realized Aidan and Young Min from previous Time class has already done something similar. [blue clock](https://studioexpanse.com/time). And the way they'd do it is almost the same as how I would imagine, with a web came or distance sensor with a linear actuator to move the clock face close or far to the front panel. I am not sure if it is a good idea to do this if someone has something similar?

But if I were to work on this, here is how my piece would be different:
- It worked the opposite as Aidan & Young Min's piece. It shows time clearly only when people is not looking at it.
	- For me, the idea is that, when you concentrate at the moment you forget about time. And also time as a concept is something that I personally feel more confused when i look into or think about it more.
- I am thinking to gives the hand some choreography when its move back and become blurry?
- Maybe try to make it work as a real wall clock -> will need to think about the camera angle.
- My clock face will look like a analog clock. Here are some of my visual inspo
