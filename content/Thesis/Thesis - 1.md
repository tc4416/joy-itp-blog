#### Concept Map
I was struggling a little bit when I am starting to draw mind maps. I have a few area that I know I am interested in and wasn't sure which should be the center of my concept map. Some I have been thinking over the summer or I have always been interested are:
- Linguistics
- Time
- Memory
- Fortune Telling / Fate
- Superstition
So I started with language and found myself extended a lots on how language connect to thoughts and linked them back to time, memory and belief. So I guess I am interested in the study of future? Some questions:
- What is an ideal future? When reality meet expectation?
- We think about our future with our own language, which, in a way, was a representation of what we have experienced and learned. Does that mean that our future is limited by our  past?
