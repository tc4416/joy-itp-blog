Title: 
*In memoriam*: 
*Memores acti prudentes futuri*:  mindful of things done, aware of things to come

Motivation:
Sometimes I go through my drive and open a file just to decide whether or not I will delete it. I look through it one last time, carefully, before its digital trace gets destroyed. That last look is the only real attention it has gotten for years. For me, there is something sacred, something close to a ritual in it. It almost feels like a funeral for something forgotten, something insignificant that used to be a tiny part of my life.

So I made this book for those files. I collected them from my personal Google Drive and two or three hard drives that I almost forgot existed. The attention arrives at the end of the file's life, and only because of the end. This book is a goodbye letter for them.
