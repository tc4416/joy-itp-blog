#### Original Proposal
[Slides](https://docs.google.com/presentation/d/13tICUG-MVbqeXQ6b_CH2A1pcNVzRpwCk3Xtme-btLLs/edit?usp=sharing)
For midterm, I wanted to make a took that can help me with my project documentation. I personally struggle it a lot to put together good documentation for my projects. Sometime, when I get into the working stage, I can't keep up with recording everything and writing down all the references I uses. So I wanted to make this tool that aim to solve this problem.

Originally, I was thinking of having a html form response type of interface that can guide the user through entering important information and progress about their project. I was inspired by an web art piece, [whenwe.love/form](https://whenwe.love/forms/), by Chia Amisola. I liked the minimalist style, and thought that having a form with different type of questions can make the documenting process les painful. However, after thinking more about it and going through another cycle of documenting my projects for my midterms from other classes, I thinking simply having a fun interface would not solve the issue. It still require me to dedicate time and a full window page to log my progress.
